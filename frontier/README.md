# Evidence frontier

Current derived views of the experimental general-capability corpus.

The frontier is live and may change as public evidence is admitted or interpretations are revised.

It must remain distinct from frozen historical synthesis snapshots preserved under `historical/`.

## Maintenance boundary

Raw public-evidence admission does not automatically mutate this layer. A newly admitted neutral observation may remain unprojected and therefore may require no frontier change yet.

This repository currently has no repository-local frontier generator/check contract. Frontier updates are therefore an explicit derived-review step after raw admission, not part of the mechanical admission gate.

Do not describe frontier refresh as automatic until generation and consistency checks are implemented in-repository.
