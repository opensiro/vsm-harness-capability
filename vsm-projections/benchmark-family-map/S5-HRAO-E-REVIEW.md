## Function-first mapping

The direct fit comes from the reported production closure path, not from the existence of a constitution or the name of a governance component:

```text
production failure or repeated governance gap
        ↓
agent learning/synthesis identifies the gap and may draft an amendment
        ↓
proposal reaches the human CEO as sole constitutional authority
        ↓
CEO ratifies, modifies or rejects; agents cannot self-ratify
        ↓
ratified amendment is committed/versioned as binding constitutional law
        ↓
all agent instances subsequently operate under the changed rule
        ↓
enforcement coverage checks amendment citation in execution logs
```

This matches the Profile's parent-governed S5 closure structure at the declared HRAO-E recursion:

1. **identity/ultimate-policy issue exists** — the reported amendment loop is reserved for constitutional governance gaps rather than ordinary task approval;
2. **legitimate ultimate authority is explicit** — the human CEO is reported as the sole constitutional ratification authority;
3. **an actual amendment path is exercised** — the production history reports ratified amendments, including named production-driven changes such as RALPH Amendment 8.6.7;
4. **ownership and enforcement are separated** — agents may identify gaps and draft proposals, but they cannot ratify; the CEO owns the constitutional decision while system machinery records and enforces the result;
5. **the decision returns to operation** — ratified amendments are reported as versioned binding law for all agent instances, with execution-log citation coverage used as a proxy for enforcement.

The observation therefore goes beyond a static constitution, policy file, approval gate or unit-tested amendment API.
